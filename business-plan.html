Toronto Painters: expansion plan

We want to open up to 50 paint branches. Each one runs the same way. We start with 2 or 3, and add more only when they work.

Parts: 1 Summary · 2 Problem · 3 How it works · 4 Market · 5 Sales and leads · 6 Money · 7 Proof and rollout · 8 Team · 9 Risks · 10 The ask · 11 Open questions

1. Summary

What we are building

  • One software (ROK) for quotes, signed contracts, invoices and receipts.
  • One agent on WhatsApp for leads and daily reports.
  • One playbook for how to price, sell and paint.

2. Problem

Why this matters

Painting companies lose leads through slow replies, and quotes and invoices are often done by hand. Each new city starts from scratch, so quality varies.

3. How it works

How a branch runs

Every branch uses the same software, the same agent and the same playbook. That makes branches easy to compare and easy to copy.

5. Sales and leads

How we get jobs

  • Advertising brings in enquiries.
  • The agent answers each enquiry and follows up the same day.
  • ROK turns each lead into a quote and a signed job.

What could go wrong
