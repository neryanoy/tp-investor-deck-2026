1. Executive summary

Toronto Painters is a painting company based in Toronto, Ontario. The plan is to open up to 50 branches, each one a copy of the same operating system: the ROK software for quotes, signed proposals, invoices and receipts, and the Toronto Painters agent for WhatsApp intake, lead follow-up and daily reporting, plus one written playbook for how to price, sell, schedule and deliver a paint job.

The idea is that every branch runs the same way, so quality and cost are predictable and the owner does not have to rebuild the process in each city. Growth is not committed all at once. The plan starts with a pilot of 2 to 3 branches for [ 90 ] days, and expands in waves only if the pilot meets three numbers (see section 7).

2. The problem and the opportunity

  • Painting companies usually sell from referrals and a small number of web enquiries, and lose leads through slow follow-up.
  • Quotes, signatures and invoices are often handled by hand, which slows the sale and makes the books hard to trust.
  • Each new location normally has to reinvent pricing, scheduling and client handling, which is where quality drifts.

The opportunity: a repeatable system that turns advertising spend into tracked leads, tracked quotes and tracked jobs, so each branch's results can be measured and compared with the others.

Market size and local demand for each city are [ to be researched ]. No figure is given here.

3. The business and how a branch runs

Each branch is the same system. Three parts:

ROK (the software). Quotes, signed proposals, invoices and receipts, with client and project tracking. Every document and status is recorded.
The agent. WhatsApp intake, lead follow-up and daily task reports, set up the same way as the main office.
The playbook. One standard way to price, sell, schedule and deliver a paint job.

Identical setup per branch keeps quality and cost predictable and makes branches comparable.

5. Go-to-market and lead generation

  • Advertising brings in enquiries. The plan needs [ 7,000$ ] per branch to launch (the basis, per branch or in total, is still to be confirmed).
  • Lead handling is done by the agent and ROK: every enquiry is recorded, answered and followed up on the same day.
  • Sales uses the standard quote and signature flow, so the time from enquiry to signed job is measured.
